Common questions

How do I cancel or change my subscription?
Open the app and go to Settings → Manage subscription, or open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

I bought Pro but the app still shows the free plan.
Open the upgrade screen and tap Restore purchase. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account you bought it with.

I forgot my password.
On the sign-in screen, enter your email and tap Forgot password. Open the link in the email on your iPhone.

How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all your events.

How do I get a copy of my data?
Go to Settings → Export your data to save your events as a spreadsheet.
